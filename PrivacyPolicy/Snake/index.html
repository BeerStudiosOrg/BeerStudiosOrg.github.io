Snake — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Snake (the “App”), a game for mobile devices created by Beer_Singh, operating as Beer Studios (“we”, “us”, “our”).

Summary

Snake does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. The game runs on your device, and we do not operate any servers that receive data from it.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The App does not require an account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal details.

We do not collect your IP address, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage statistics, or crash reports.

Data stored on your device

The App may save game-related data, such as your high score and settings, locally on your device. This data stays on your device, is not sent to us or to anyone else, and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

Third-party services

The App is built with the Unity game engine. The App does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or social-media SDKs, and we have not enabled Unity Analytics, Unity Ads, or any other online service from Unity or other providers.

The store you download the App from (for example, Google Play or the Apple App Store) may collect information about your download and device under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or control that information.

Permissions

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, files, or any other sensitive device feature.

Sharing of information

Because we do not collect personal information, we have nothing to sell, rent, or share with third parties.

Children’s privacy

Snake is a general-audience game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. If you have questions about your privacy or want to confirm this, you can contact us at any time.

Security

The App does not transmit personal data off your device, which keeps your information under your control. Your device’s own security settings protect the locally stored game data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If we add features that collect data (such as ads or analytics), we will update this policy before releasing them.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, email us at beerstudios2093@gmail.com.
